# Store listing — English

**Name:** Dr. Bunny — Hearing Loss Simulator
**Subtitle / Short description:** Hear how hearing loss sounds

## Description
Dr. Bunny lets you *feel* and *understand* hearing loss with an audiogram you control.

• **Interactive audiogram** — drag thresholds at 6 frequencies; sound icons turn gray when you can no longer hear them.
• **Letters drop out by frequency** — high-pitched consonants like s, x, th disappear first; see a conversation lose letters at 4 severity levels (Vietnamese and English).
• **Background noise** — supermarket, restaurant, car… and how noise masks weak consonants.
• **Dr. Bunny chat** — answers based on your own audiogram, with tips for talking to someone with hearing loss.
• **Runs entirely on your device** — no network, no account, no data collection.

**Note:** an educational simulation, not a medical device. It does not diagnose and does not replace a professional hearing test. Turn the volume down before you listen.
